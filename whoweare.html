Mission

Sustain Habits is an HR agency specialized in seasonal employment within the hotel industry. Our mission is to create meaningful connections between young professionals and hospitality businesses, while promoting sustainability as a core value in tourism. We support workers not only in finding jobs, but in building long-term, eco-conscious careers.

Services

  • AI-based job matchmaking for seasonal hotel roles
  • Skill development & short-term training (soft & hard skills)
  • Onboarding and coaching for career advancement
  • Certified sustainability training for hotel teams
  • Staff leasing and talent acquisition for 4* & 5* resorts
  • Winter/summer dual-season job placement

Team

Our team brings together expertise from HR, tourism, and sustainability. We are a group of professionals passionate about shaping a new, more conscious workforce for the hospitality sector.
